# Architectural Decision Record (ADR): Focus Score Calculation Using a Fixed 10-Minute Denominator

## Status
Accepted

## Context
In previous versions of tenby10, the focus score for a 10-minute slot was calculated relative to the number of *logged* segments in that slot. If a slot contained only 1 log entry (e.g., because the computer was asleep or off for the other 9 minutes), and that 1 log was active and productive, the slot was scored as 100% focused.

This approach presented several functional and visual problems:
1. **Visual Metaphor Inflation:** A slot with only 1 minute of active work would occupy an entire 10-minute column on the timeline as a solid green 100% focus card, misleading users and clients.
2. **Context-Switching Loophole:** It enabled users to "game" the focus metrics by working for a brief 30-second window to log a productive minute and then closing the lid or turning off the daemon, locking in a 100% score.
3. **Mismatched Deep-Work Goals:** From an effectiveness perspective, a 10-minute block that only yields 1 minute of focused attention represents low net effectiveness (10%), even if that 1 minute was highly focused.

## Decision
We will calculate focus scores using a fixed **10-minute denominator** (10 segments) for all slots that contain at least 1 minute log.

1. **Static Engine Calculation:**
   The focus score will be computed as:
   $$\text{Focus Score} = \frac{\text{Productive Segments} \times 100}{10}$$
   Where $\text{Productive Segments}$ includes minutes classified as `Active`, `PassiveReview`, and `Meeting`.

2. **LLM Engine Alignment:**
   To prevent engine-switching drift, any focus score generated by the LLM for a partial slot will be scaled by the ratio of logged minutes:
   $$\text{Final Focus Score} = \text{LLM Focus Score} \times \frac{\text{Logged Minutes}}{10}$$

3. **Exemption for Offline Slots:**
   If a slot contains exactly 0 logged minutes, it remains classified as `Offline` (greyed out) and does not write a 0% focus summary to the ledger, preventing artificial penalty on long periods of legitimate away-time.

## Consequences
* **Deep Work Reinforcement:** Short context-switches (opening the laptop for 1 minute to check an alert) are visually and mathematically penalized (scored as 10%), training users to batch communication tasks and protect their flow state.
* **Elimination of the "Offline Silence" Cheat:** Users can no longer maintain a high focus score card by turning off the tracking daemon during periods of low productivity.
* **Boundary Noise Acceptance:** Slots at the boundary of a workday (startup or shutdown) will reflect a lower score due to partial-slot usage, which is accepted as a minor and honest trade-off for overall system integrity.
